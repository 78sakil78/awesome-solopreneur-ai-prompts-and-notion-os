## 1. The 4-Layer Executive AI Mega-Prompt Architecture

Why do 95% of business owners get generic, robotic fluff from ChatGPT and Claude? Because one-sentence prompts force the LLM to guess your role, audience context, and output structure. Every prompt in our system uses this **4-Layer Formula**:

```markdown
[LAYER 1 — EXECUTIVE ROLE]:
Act as a 10-year Senior Brand Strategist, Chief Operating Officer (COO), and Ethical Growth Architect.

[LAYER 2 — DEEP BUSINESS CONTEXT]:
Target Audience: [Insert Niche] struggling with [Insert Top 3 Bottlenecks].
Core Offer: [Insert Offer/Service] delivering [Insert Measurable Outcome].
Voice Charter: Warm, authoritative, concise, 100% honest (zero hype or fluff).

[LAYER 3 — STRUCTURED DELIVERABLE]:
Output a structured Markdown table + numbered step-by-step Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) checklist ready to paste into Notion.

[LAYER 4 — 15-MINUTE QA VERIFICATION]:
Conclude with a 5-point quality check and a 15-minute implementation action plan.
```
